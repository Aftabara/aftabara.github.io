Dr. Aftab Ara

Ph.D. in Management. Researcher and author on responsible AI, digital transformation of universities, and people-centred sustainability.

I study how organisations and universities can adopt AI in ways that are useful, accountable and human-centred. My work spans research and writing on AI ethics, generative AI in higher education, HR and knowledge management, business analytics and green HRM; teaching business, analytics and entrepreneurship courses with AI built into the classroom; designing curricula and running faculty workshops; contributing to IEEE-EDU P3722, the draft guide to responsible AI in higher education; and serving as an editor, reviewer and research coordinator.

About

Research that connects knowledge with practice

My doctoral work (Ravenshaw University, 2016) examined human resource practices and knowledge management in universities. Since then I have moved toward the questions universities and companies now face: how to adopt generative AI without losing trust, equity or academic integrity, and how people and institutions can reach sustainability goals.

I coordinate research with the Global Sustainable Futures Network across the MENA region and South Asia, and I am a working member who contributed to the draft of IEEE-EDU P3722, the guide for responsible AI in higher education.

Education: Ph.D. in Management, Ravenshaw University (2016).

Teaching

Teaching and training

Twenty years of university teaching, most recently at the University of Hail (2023–2026). I teach business analytics, entrepreneurship, organisational behaviour, human resource management, strategy, operations and project management, using cases, simulations and student projects, with AI used for assessments and research tasks and with students kept in charge of their own work.

  • Coordinated courses and curricula in compensation management, analytics, entrepreneurship and business English; member of the Curriculum Development Committee.
  • Faculty workshops: Generative AI Applications and Research Areas in Business Disciplines (February 2025); Empowering Entrepreneurship (February 2024); Conducting Comprehensive Research (June 2023).
  • Research methods and tools: SPSS, SmartPLS (structural equation modelling), NVivo.
